---
name: Payroll and attendance closure scope
description: User-required joint payroll/attendance closure and administrator-only month controls.
---

“цалин дээр сар хаахад давхар ирц хаагддаг байх. сарыг админаас өөр хэн ч нээж хаах эрхгүй байх”

**Why:** The user requires attendance to close together with payroll, and reserves both closing and reopening for administrators.

**How to apply:** Preserve the shared effective payroll-period boundary rather than introducing a separate attendance close/reopen workflow. Accountants and HR may retain their ordinary work permissions, but must not gain month-closing or reopening rights.
