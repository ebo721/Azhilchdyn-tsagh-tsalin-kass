---
name: Fixed insurance bases
description: Full deductions from insurance salary below configured base salary, plus the 792,000₮ exception.
---

НДШ тооцох цалин нь 792,000₮ ажилтны НДШ, ХХОАТ-ыг ажилласан хоногоор хувьчлахгүй, бүтэн 792,000₮-өөс бодно. Огт ажиллаагүй бол суутгахгүй.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the full minimum-wage insurance base and chose “Ажиллаагүй бол суутгахгүй” when asked about months without work.

**How to apply:** This is an exception for the configured effective insurance base, not a minimum floor applied to every employee's prorated insurance salary. Preserve actual gross earnings, tax exemptions, effective salary history and recorded payments. Use the existing tax rates and relief. Do not silently recalculate saved or closed months.

НДШ төлөх цалин нь тохируулсан үндсэн цалингаас бага тохиолдолд НДШ, ХХОАТ-ыг НДШ төлөх цалингийн дүнгээр бүтэн суутгана.

**Why:** The user broadened the rule beyond 792,000₮ and explicitly chose “Тохируулсан үндсэн цалинтай”, not the month's attendance-prorated earnings, as the comparison.

**How to apply:** Compare the effective configured amounts, not earned gross and not today's settings for a past month. A month with no eligible worked attendance remains untaxed for this fixed-base rule. Actual gross, paid amounts and closed/saved calculations retain their existing protections.

When the insurance salary stays unchanged and non-exempt across a base-salary change, eligible worked attendance under the lower-insurance rule makes that insurance base apply once, in full, for the month.

**Why:** Splitting one unchanged insurance base between ordinary attendance proration and a fixed portion can exceed the configured insurance salary when a mid-month raise changes the comparison.

**How to apply:** Do not add an earlier prorated portion to the same fixed insurance base. Keep genuine insurance-base changes and exemption changes effective-dated; a later raise without eligible work under that rate must not retroactively qualify earlier attendance.
